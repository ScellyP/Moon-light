## Highlights
- **Change your dock order** — new option in Settings > Appearance > Dock order: drag the items into the order you like and press Done. Swiping between screens follows it, and it stays after you restart the app
- **Download notification** — the progress bar now keeps moving while a song is preparing, and the X in the Download songs header is bigger
- **Personalised Home greeting** — the app now shows your name right on the home page
- **Name input polish** — left-aligned, capped at 16 characters for a cleaner look

## Previous highlights
- **Dynamic notification colours** that match your playing cover art or active theme
- **Background & resumable downloads** — single-song and playlist downloads keep running across tab switches and app restarts
- **Better slide animations** with more control over tab switching
- **Dock changes** — Search moved to the middle
