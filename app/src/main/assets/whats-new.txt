## Highlights
- **Dynamic notification colours** that match your playing cover art or active theme
- **Background & resumable downloads** — single-song and playlist downloads keep running across tab switches and app restarts
- **Names** The app now remembers your name locally no data is send out or used and is just displayed in home 
page 
- **Better slide animations** The slide now uses a new transition effect instead of just switching slides directly now you have more control over switching tabs swiping thingy
- **Dock changes** Moved the search to the middle. *This will be changed in the next patch where the dock positions will be made customisable in settings.* 
  
## Improvements
- **Sharper moon phase badge** with a crisp circular ring and automatic hourly updates from system time
- **Cleaner search history** — Search now keeps only your latest 3 recent searches
